01 / THE MISSION

What you're signing up for.
Real impact, genuine accountability.

Becoming a core team member is not an honorary credential for your LinkedIn headline. It is a commitment to lead, execute under pressure, and create something durable.

Pillar 01

Absolute Ownership

Core members do not wait for instruction sheets. You identify bottlenecks, propose bold solutions, take the reins, and see projects through to completion.

No Micro-management
Pillar 02

Execution Under Pressure

Hackathons, product launches, workshops, and high-visibility community events will have real deadlines. You stay calm, organized, and focused on delivery.

Real-world Resilience
Pillar 03

Consistent Contribution

A great sprint followed by weeks of silence hurts the team. We value students who show up reliably week in and week out, communicating clearly and supporting peers.

Reliability Above All
02 / THE TEAMS

Four available roles.
Find where you belong.

Read the responsibilities for each team thoroughly before submitting your application. Every role has defined ownership and plays a pivotal part in the chapter.

Team 01

TECHNICAL TEAM

Responsible for the technical side of the chapter. This includes working on coding and development, building technical projects, maintaining technical infrastructure where required, solving technical problems, and contributing to the development side of OS Code initiatives.

Hands-on software development & open-source projects
Maintaining chapter repos, tools, and technical infrastructure
Designing coding challenges, workshops, and tech reviews
Team 02

SOCIAL MEDIA TEAM

Responsible for building and maintaining the chapter's online presence. This includes content creation, managing social media platforms, growing the community's reach and followers, documenting activities, creating posts/reels/other content, and keeping the community engaged and active online.

Reels, short-form video editing, and visual storytelling
Managing LinkedIn, Instagram, and community platform updates
Live event coverage, photography, and audience interaction
Team 03

MARKETING TEAM

Responsible for promoting OS Code activities and helping increase participation and visibility. This includes outreach, promotion, communication, creating awareness around events and initiatives, connecting with students and communities, and developing strategies to increase engagement.

Campus outreach, classroom tours, and registration campaigns
Connecting with external developer clubs & student bodies
Strategic communication and retention campaigns for events
Team 04

EVENT MANAGEMENT TEAM

Responsible for planning and executing events and activities. This includes planning events, coordinating with teams, handling logistics, obtaining necessary approvals, coordinating with relevant college authorities, managing execution on the day of the event, and ensuring that events run smoothly.

Coordination with faculty, department heads, and venue staff
On-ground logistics, A/V setups, seating, and schedule adherence
Speaker hospitality, stage management, and crisis mitigation
03 / EXPECTATIONS

The Code of Conduct.

Joining our core means holding yourself to a higher standard. Here is what we expect from day one.

01

Integrity & Accountability

When you accept an assignment or event deliverable, your peers depend on you. If an obstacle emerges, you raise a flag immediately rather than letting deadlines slip silently.

02

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Teams are not silos. The Tech team helps Event Management with registration software; Social Media works with Marketing on campaigns; everyone helps pitch in during flagship chapter events.

03

Continuous Learning & Curiosity

You don't need to know every technology or skill today, but you must possess an intense willingness to learn fast, absorb feedback, and push your limits.

04

Student Community First

Our mission is to empower student developers at East Point College of Engineering and Technology. Every workshop, event, and hackathon must create genuine value for the participants.
